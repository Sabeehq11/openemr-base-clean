## Blood pressure categories
Normal blood pressure is below 120/80 mm Hg. Stage 1 hypertension is a systolic pressure of 130 to 139 or a diastolic pressure of 80 to 89. Stage 2 is 140/90 or higher. The diagnosis rests on an average of two or more careful readings on at least two occasions.

## Treatment goal
For adults with confirmed hypertension and known cardiovascular disease, or a high estimated risk, a blood pressure goal below 130/80 mm Hg is recommended. The same goal is generally used for adults with diabetes or chronic kidney disease.

## First-line medicine classes
First-line classes are thiazide diuretics, calcium channel blockers, and ACE inhibitors or angiotensin receptor blockers. An ACE inhibitor or angiotensin receptor blocker is favored when the person also has diabetes with albuminuria or chronic kidney disease.

## Follow-up after a change
After starting or changing blood pressure treatment, follow-up is about monthly until the goal is reached. Electrolytes and kidney function are checked after starting a diuretic, ACE inhibitor or angiotensin receptor blocker.
