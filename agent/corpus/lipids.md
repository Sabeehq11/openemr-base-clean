## Statin therapy in adults with diabetes
Adults aged 40 to 75 with diabetes are advised to take a moderate-intensity statin regardless of their LDL cholesterol level. A high-intensity statin is reasonable when there are multiple risk factors or the person is older within this range.

## LDL cholesterol targets in established cardiovascular disease
In people with clinical atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, a high-intensity or maximally tolerated statin is the first step. If LDL cholesterol stays at or above 70 mg/dL on the maximal statin, adding a non-statin medicine such as ezetimibe is reasonable.

## Lipid panel monitoring
A fasting or nonfasting lipid panel is repeated 4 to 12 weeks after starting or changing statin therapy to check adherence and response, and then every 3 to 12 months. A nonfasting sample is acceptable for most people.

## Liver and muscle checks on statins
A baseline liver panel is checked before starting a statin. Routine repeat liver tests are not needed unless symptoms appear. Muscle symptoms are assessed before a statin is stopped, and creatine kinase is measured when symptoms are significant.
